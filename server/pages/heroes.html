Hero Experience

Every historic hero you can hunt down and recruit, and how a city's mayor earns experience.

These famous generals hide out in barbarian camps across the map. Hear of them at the Inn (Folk Hearsay), get a hero's portrait with Enquire Details, then bring that camp's loyalty to 0 with the portrait in hand: the hero is captured into your Feasting Hall to be persuaded. The attributes below are each hero's own, before the points it gains by level.

Mayor XP — building your city

The hero you appoint as a city's Mayor (Chief) earns experience every time the city finishes something — a building upgrade, a trained troop, or a wall defence. The XP is drawn from both what it cost and how long it takes to make:

Mayor XP = {{MAYOR_RATE}} × ( food + wood + stone + iron + build seconds )
